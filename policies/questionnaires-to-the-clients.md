## Ballarat Grammar

What specific data security measures and privacy protocols are in place to protect donors' personal information, and how does Charidy comply with relevant data protection legislation?&#x20;

In response to your inquiry about the specific data security measures and privacy protocols we employ to protect donors' personal information, as well as our compliance with relevant data protection legislation, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and security. Our platform ensures the protection of donor information through stringent encryption methods for data at rest and in transit, reinforcing the confidentiality and integrity of sensitive information. We implement robust access controls, restricting data access to authorized personnel only, thereby safeguarding against unauthorized access or breaches. On request, we can anonymize or pseudonymize donor data, further enhancing privacy. Moreover, our compliance framework encompasses adherence to major data protection regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for our European Union donors, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for residents of California, and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) for secure payment processing. These measures and protocols collectively ensure a comprehensive approach to data security, privacy, and regulatory compliance, providing our donors with the assurance that their personal information is protected with the utmost care and diligence.

Can you outline Charidy's uptime guarantees and the contingency plans for technical failures or service disruptions, especially during high-traffic fundraising events?&#x20;

Charidy has 99.95% uptime over past 12 months, ensuring our platform remains highly available and reliable, especially during critical high-traffic fundraising events. To safeguard against unforeseen technical issues, we have comprehensive contingency strategies in place, including a robust Data Backup and Recovery Plan that enables us to quickly restore services in the event of data loss or corruption. Our infrastructure includes Redundant Systems designed to prevent downtime by seamlessly taking over should any component fail. An Emergency Communication Plan is activated immediately to inform and guide our users in the rare event of a disruption, ensuring transparency and continuous updates. Furthermore, we employ a DDoS Protection Plan to mitigate the risk of distributed denial-of-service attacks, which are increasingly common during peak activity periods. These measures collectively ensure that Charidy remains resilient, secure, and operational, providing our users with confidence in our platform's reliability during their most crucial fundraising initiatives.

**Q5 b. What is your policy around who can access our systems?**\
Our policy regarding access to systems is comprehensive and designed to ensure security and privacy. Here are the key points:

* **Access Control:** Access to systems is restricted based on job role, necessity, and authorization. Each user is assigned unique credentials (such as username and password) for authentication. Access levels are determined by management and IT security, following the principle of least privilege.
* **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is implemented for added security. Users must regularly update their passwords to maintain system integrity. Strong password policies are enforced to prevent unauthorized access.
* **Monitoring and Logging:** System access activities are logged and monitored for security purposes. Any suspicious or unauthorized access attempts are promptly investigated.
* **Training and Awareness:** Employees receive training on security best practices and data handling procedures. Regular awareness campaigns are conducted to educate users about potential security threats and how to mitigate them.
* **Compliance:** Our access control policies adhere to relevant regulatory requirements and industry standards. Regular audits and assessments are conducted to ensure compliance and identify areas for improvement.
* **Incident Response:** A robust incident response plan is in place to address any security breaches or unauthorized access incidents. Procedures are documented and regularly tested to minimize the impact of security incidents.
* **Continuous Improvement:** We regularly review and update our access control policies and procedures to adapt to evolving security threats and technology advancements. Feedback from employees and security experts is incorporated to enhance our security posture.

***

**Q5 c. How do you ensure all access is authorized?**\
At Charidy, we have several access security and monitoring controls in place to protect against internal and external risks and ensure personal information is only accessed by authorized persons. Here are some examples:

1. **User Authentication:** Implementing user authentication measures such as strong passwords and multi-factor authentication (MFA) helps ensure that only authorized personnel can access sensitive data.
2. **Access Control:** Role-based access control limits access to sensitive data to only those employees who require it for their job functions.
3. **Monitoring and Logging:** A system to monitor access and activities on the network, such as logging, auditing, and tracking, helps identify suspicious behavior or unauthorized access attempts.
